Aequum Solutions

A market this size shouldn't still run on spreadsheets.

Meet the operating system for political campaigns.

There are more than 500,000 elected offices in the United States. 96% are local. Most local campaigns still operate with insufficient tools. They're tracking field operations in Excel, managing compliance filings by hand, and coordinating volunteers over group texts.

A3Q is replacing today's patchwork of disconnected tools with one unified platform.

Origin story

This is what happens when a talented software engineer's mom decides to run for office.

Adil Patel was at AWS when his mother ran for local office. He watched her wrestle with disconnected campaign tools: spreadsheets, group texts, manual compliance filings, clunky canvassing tools, all with no operational visibility and constant fear of missing required filings. So he built her a better system.

Other campaigns saw it and asked to use it. That's when he saw that legacy tools are built for the top 1% of federal races, and even those campaigns are stitching together a dozen disconnected products. Everyone else has nothing. A3Q is building an answer for all.
