For faculty and programs

Bring campaign operations into your course.

You set the academic goal. We set up the mock campaign, or campaigns if you want different groups in the class to compete, and provide professional campaign consultants who can teach from real world experience. An engagement can be a single guest session, a workshop, a multi-week project, or a semester capstone.

Our origin

It started with a problem.

When his mother ran for local office, AWS engineer Adil Patel saw how much effort went into keeping a political campaign organized. The software that existed was built for campaigns a hundred times the size, and priced to match.

That experience became A3Q's foundation, and the company is still built here, by a team with deep ties to Birmingham and to Alabama.

Let's work together to put more Alabama-based talent, from the students themselves to the civic work they'll run, at the forefront of the political scene.

Build real world experience on your campus.
